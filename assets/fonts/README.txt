Put an Arabic-capable TTF font here, named exactly:
  Cairo-Regular.ttf
  Cairo-Bold.ttf

Download "Cairo" (free, OFL license) from Google Fonts:
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cairo

Without these two files the app still runs and PDFs still generate,
but Arabic text in the contract PDF will not render correctly
(the warning banner in the PDF preview screen tells you this).
